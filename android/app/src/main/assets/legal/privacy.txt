Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KibbiSave (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our group-savings app and related websites in Uganda. It is written in plain language so members, organisers, and visitors can understand what happens to their data.

Public encyclopedic and legal summaries of data protection generally describe personal data as information that identifies or can identify a person — for example a name, phone number, email, or online identifier. Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 sets out principles for lawful, fair, and secure processing. We design our practices with awareness of those themes. This notice is informational and does not replace formal registrations, filings, or advice that may be required as the product scales.

Who this policy applies to

People who create KibbiSave accounts

Members and organisers of savings groups

Visitors who browse marketing or help pages

People who contact support

Information we collect

Account and profile

Name, display name, email, phone number

Password (stored in a protected form, not as plain text)

Profile photo or avatar you upload

Preferences such as dark mode

Group and savings activity

Groups you create, join, or leave

Contribution amounts, timestamps, and status (pending, confirmed, failed)

Leaderboard and standing information derived from activity

Invite codes you create or use (as needed to operate groups)

Payments (limited)

Payment providers process Mobile Money or other transfers under their own terms. We may receive confirmation references, amounts, status, and identifiers needed to match a payment to your account and group. We do not need your Mobile Money PIN, and you should never send PINs or OTPs to us.

Device, session, and technical data

Login sessions and authentication cookies / tokens

Basic device and browser information

IP address and approximate logs used for security and diagnostics

Error reports and performance signals

Support communications

If you email or message support, we keep the content needed to resolve your request, including attachments you choose to send (avoid sending secrets).

How we use information

Operate accounts, groups, deposits, and history

Show rankings, progress, and member lists within groups as designed

Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access

Improve reliability, usability, and customer support

Send service notices (for example security or material terms updates)

Comply with law where we are legally required to do so

We do not sell your personal data.

Legal and principle themes (Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019)

At a high level, Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 is commonly described as requiring personal data to be processed lawfully, for specified purposes, with appropriate security, and with respect for data-subject rights. Themes often highlighted in public summaries include:

Collecting data for clear purposes related to the service

Not keeping data longer than needed for those purposes (subject to legal retention needs)

Taking reasonable steps to keep data accurate and secure

Respecting rights of access and correction where applicable

Being careful with sensitive categories of data

Citation (informational reference): Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 — themes summarised for education; not a full legal commentary and not a claim of completed regulatory filings.

Sharing

We may share limited data with:

Payment providers — to initiate or confirm deposits

Hosting and infrastructure vendors — to run the app securely

Other group members — profile and activity visibility that is inherent to group savings (for example names and contribution standing inside a group)

Authorities — when required by applicable law, court order, or lawful request

Professional advisers — under confidentiality, where needed for legal or compliance work

If we ever use analytics or marketing tools that set additional cookies, we will update the Cookies Policy and this notice as needed.

Visibility inside groups

Group savings only works if members can see enough information to trust the circle. That usually includes display names and contribution progress. Do not join a group if you are uncomfortable with that level of visibility. Organisers should not publish member lists outside the app without a lawful basis and member awareness.

Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to a consumer app — such as encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls, and protected password storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. You also play a role: protect your password, device lock, SIM, and Mobile Money PIN.

Bank of Uganda consumer-awareness themes for digital payments often stress credential protection and caution against social engineering. Those habits protect privacy as well as money.

Citation (informational reference): BoU payment-security awareness themes — educational only.

Retention

We keep account and transaction records for as long as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, meet bookkeeping or legal obligations, and improve security. When data is no longer needed, we aim to delete or anonymise it where practical.

Children

KibbiSave is intended for adults who can enter binding agreements and manage money responsibly. If you believe a child has created an account inappropriately, contact us so we can review.

International processing

Infrastructure providers may process data in more than one country. Where that occurs, we seek appropriate safeguards consistent with our security and privacy goals and applicable law.

Your choices and rights

Update profile details in the app

Request access to or correction of personal data we hold, where applicable

Request deletion where the law allows and where we do not need to retain data for legal or security reasons

Clear cookies and site storage in your browser (see Cookies Policy)

To make a privacy request, use the contact channel on the About page or in-app support. We may need to verify it is really you before fulfilling the request.

Anti-money-laundering and integrity checks

Themes in Uganda’s Anti-Money Laundering framework commonly include knowing your customer and monitoring for suspicious activity. We may request additional verification or restrict accounts where integrity checks require it. This is about platform safety, not an accusation against ordinary savers.

Citation (informational reference): Anti-Money Laundering Act themes — educational only.

Data minimisation in practice

We aim to collect what we need to run groups and deposits — not every possible data point. Organisers should follow the same spirit: do not demand unnecessary personal documents in chat, and do not publish member phone numbers outside the circle without a clear reason and member awareness.

World Bank consumer-protection discussions around digital finance often link privacy to trust: people use savings tools more confidently when they understand who sees their information. That is why this policy separates account data, group visibility, and payment confirmations.

Breach awareness

If we become aware of a security incident that materially affects your personal data, we will take steps consistent with applicable law and good practice — including containment, assessment, and user or authority notification where required. You can reduce impact by using unique passwords and enabling device locks.

Marketing communications

Service messages (security, material terms changes, deposit confirmations where applicable) are part of operating the product. Optional product news or tips, if introduced, will be distinguishable where practical, and you may be able to opt out of non-essential marketing without losing core account access.

Automated decisions

We may use automated signals (for example repeated failed logins or unusual deposit patterns) to protect accounts. These are integrity and fraud-prevention tools, not credit scoring for bank loans. If an automated control restricts your account, contact support for a human review path where available.

Group organisers and other people’s data

If you administer a group, you may see member names and contribution activity needed to run the circle. Treat that information as confidential to the group. Do not export member lists for spam, shaming, or sale. Public data-protection themes emphasise purpose limitation — use member data for running the savings group, not unrelated commercial exploitation.

Members should also respect each other’s privacy in chats and screenshots. Visibility inside a group is for accountability, not for public humiliation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product evolves. The “Last updated” date will change, and material updates may also be highlighted in News or in-app notices.
